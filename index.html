behind ranamgo
Vishnu GS, Creator & Game Designer of RanamGo
Kerala, India
I am an independent creator with an interest in exploring ideas across multiple fields. RanamGo is one of the ideas I conceived and developed, drawing inspiration from the Panchabhootha concept - the five elements of nature, which I interpreted as special Power elements within the game.
What began as an idea evolved into a two-player card game built around luck, tactical decisions, and Power Attacks.
Independently conceived and developed in India.
