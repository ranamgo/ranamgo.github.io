The regular battle is over!

Both players have the same number of Resting Cards. It's time to settle the game through Sudden Death!

HOW IT WORKS

  • Both players use their Resting Cards as their new Combat decks.
  • Both players reveal one Combat Card in each battle.
  • Power Attacks are allowed when enough Power Cards are available.
  • If a Power Attack is unavailable, the higher-value Combat Card wins.
  • If a Power Attack ends in a tie, the battle continues.
  • The first player to win a battle wins the game! (Higher-valued card wins the game)
